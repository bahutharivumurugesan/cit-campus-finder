# Quick student survey

You can ask these questions in person or through a form. Keep it to about two minutes.

1. Are you a CIT student, staff member, or visitor? (Optional)
2. Which campus place have you found difficult to locate? (Open answer)
3. When was the last time you needed to find it?
4. How did you find it? (Asked someone / followed signs / searched online / other)
5. About how long did it take?
6. Which would help most? (Search by place / directions from a selected starting point / floor details / accessible route / other)
7. Would you try a simple campus-finder website? What would make it useful?

Ask before recording anyone's name, photo, or quote. For the project report, summarize responses without identifying people unless they have agreed.
