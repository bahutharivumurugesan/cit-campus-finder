# LinkedIn post draft

I’m working on a small project to solve a problem students can run into on campus: finding the right place quickly.

My project, **CIT Campus Finder**, is a searchable website concept for Chennai Institute of Technology. A student can look up a place and see its building, floor, nearby landmark, and a short direction clue.

I’ve built an early prototype with search, categories, and a clickable campus overview. The next step is to speak with CIT students, verify the locations, and improve the directions based on how people actually move around campus.

This is still a student project prototype, not an official CIT map. I’m looking for feedback from students: **Which place on campus was hardest for you to find when you first joined?**
