# Public research notes

Checked public web results on 17 November 2026 to understand what CIT campus content and place names are visible. Public videos and posts are discovery clues only; they do not verify exact routes, room numbers, or current facility names.

## What appeared in public content

- A public CIT day-in-the-life post mentions food options, classes, labs, lunch, sports, and a hostel boundary. It shows that a campus finder could cover more than academic rooms; hostel areas should be treated as access-controlled.
- A YouTube campus tour result exists, but it is a general overview and does not provide a verified indoor floor map.
- CIT's official site describes the campus and facilities, including the Arusuvai Food Court. Its campus reports identify places such as Main Block, First Year Block, Auditorium, Canteen, and Mess Hall.
- A public Reddit result titled “CIT Maps — an indoor navigation app” claims a similar app with floor maps, search, chatbot, and offline navigation. Treat that claim as a signal to check whether a project already exists, not as confirmed product research. Ask students and the post author whether it is active and what gap remains before presenting this as a novel idea.

## How to use these findings

Use public media to decide what to ask students about, not to copy media or infer a route from a camera angle. Ask CIT students to verify names, building entrances, floors, accessible paths, and whether the existing CIT Maps project is still available. Get permission before using official logos, student photos, or identifiable quotes in the project or social posts.
